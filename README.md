I build and share free tools for car enthusiasts. Most of my work here is around BMW iDrive, from organizing service records to exploring what factory infotainment systems can do. You’ll find my own projects alongside forks, Android ports, and contributions to other people’s work.

### 🛠️ Service and maintenance

- **[BMW Service History Manager](https://github.com/statist1c/BMW-Service-History-Manager)** · A native Android app to create, edit, and organize BMW service records, with JSON and Toolset32 import and export.

### 📱 Android Auto

- **[BMW CIC Android Auto](https://github.com/statist1c/bmw-cic-androidauto)** · A wired Android Auto plugin for BMW CIC HIGH that uses the factory screen, audio system, and iDrive controller.
- **[Supra iDrive 6 Android Auto](https://github.com/statist1c/Supra-iDrive6-Android-Auto)** · A fork of Sq00ky’s Android Auto project for the Toyota Supra’s NBT Evo head unit.

### 🎨 iDrive apps and customization

- **[KurdBimmer Cluster Themes](https://github.com/statist1c/KB-cluster-themes)** · FDX100’s collection of BMW iDrive 7 instrument cluster themes, with a Sorani Kurdish interface.
- **[MGU Store](https://github.com/statist1c/mgu-store)** · An app catalog for browsing and managing apps on BMW iDrive 7 and 8.
- **[MGU Store Installer](https://github.com/statist1c/MGU-Store-Installer)** · An experimental Android companion for setting up robertnisipeanu’s MGU Store.
- **[MGU FSC Generator by Noahbg](https://github.com/statist1c/MGU-FSC-Generator-by-Noahbg)** · An experimental Android port of Noahbg’s BMW MGU utility.

### 🧪 iDrive 7 and 8 experiments

- **iDrive 7/8 SSH Root** · A BMW MGU head-unit research project.
- **MGU iDrive 7/8 Root Companion** · An experimental Android companion for BMW MGU research.

If you try one of these projects, I’d love to hear what worked and what could be better. 🚗
